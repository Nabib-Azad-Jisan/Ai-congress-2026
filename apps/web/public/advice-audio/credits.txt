AgroDoctor spoken guidance

The MP3s in this directory were synthesized from AgroDoctor's fixed bilingual
guidance sentences using the offline Piper 1.8.0 engine. The build script is
scripts/generate_doctor_speech.py. Both ONNX voices were obtained from
rhasspy/piper-voices at revision 04ca88f5685ca5253c701af3687e155b113bd512.

Bengali

bn_BD-google-medium.onnx, speaker 0. Voice card and upstream notices:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/04ca88f5685ca5253c701af3687e155b113bd512/bn/bn_BD/google/medium/MODEL_CARD

Based on the Bengali TTS dataset collected by Google (OpenSLR SLR37) and the
CMU Indic speech database. The upstream model card credits the Hi-Fi TTS base
model, reader 92, Cori Samuel. The original audio was used to train the voice;
these clips are newly synthesized readings of AgroDoctor guidance.
https://www.openslr.org/37/
http://festvox.org/cmu_indic/

The Bengali MP3s are provided under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International. Attribute AgroDoctor, the Piper bn_BD-google-medium voice,
Google OpenSLR SLR37, and CMU Indic when redistributing these audio files.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

English

en_US-ljspeech-medium.onnx. The upstream model was trained by Bryce Beattie
from the public-domain LJ Speech Dataset compiled by Keith Ito and Linda Johnson.
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/04ca88f5685ca5253c701af3687e155b113bd512/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium/MODEL_CARD
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
